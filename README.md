### Tips & Advices
📖 There are some tips for you, hope they're useful for you:
- 💬 **Language**: Our website's default language is **Simplified Chinese**, you can click the button **⚙️ 设置** or enter URL `omg-by-clark.github.io/settings.html` and switch language to English.
- 📝 **Version codename**: Our version codenames are **drink names**, the version codename is currently **🥤 Coca-Cola**.
- 🌓 **Display mode**: If you want to **switch the display mode** of our website, you can also go to settings page and find the second select box, and switch it.
- 🚩 **Post**: If you want to post your stories, you can click the button **🤬 Post Now**, but make sure your post is **exactly your upset or angry life story**.
- 💻 **Code**: If you want to use our code, of **course**! **But**, please comply with **GNU Affero General Public License** (file `LICENSE`).
- 📢 **Advice**: Have advice? Send your advice to `omg-clarkzhang[-a-t-]outlook.com` (`[-a-t-]` means `@`), you also can open an issue.
